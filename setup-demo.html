One-time demo account setup

This creates the two permanent demo accounts (Demo Admin, Demo User) in Firebase Authentication and their profile documents in Firestore. Safe to tap more than once — if the accounts already exist, it just confirms they work instead of erroring out.

Accounts ready. Next: copy these two UIDs into firebase/firestore.rules, replacing REPLACE_WITH_DEMO_ADMIN_UID and REPLACE_WITH_DEMO_USER_UID, then publish the rules in Firebase Console. After that, log in as Demo Admin and click "Seed Demo Data" in Settings.

This page isn't linked from anywhere in the app — bookmark or remember this URL if you need to run it again later.
